About Our Club

37+ Years of Dedicated Service in JP Nagar

Rotary Bangalore JP Nagar was chartered on January 4, 1989, as part of Rotary International District 3191. Our club unites business leaders, accomplished professionals, and community changemakers passionate about creating enduring change.

Under the leadership of President Rtn. PHF G M Ravi for Rotary Year 2026-27, we embrace the call to "Create Lasting Impact" through healthcare interventions, school development, youth empowerment, and ecological restoration.

Community Impact

Participate in life-saving projects including pediatric surgeries, school support, and clean water drives.

Leadership Growth

Lead committees, manage major initiatives, and hone public speaking and governance skills.

Global Fellowship

Connect with 1.2+ million Rotarians across 35,000+ clubs worldwide with open doors in 200+ countries.

Vocational Network

Build trusted professional relationships based on the Rotary Four-Way Test of truth and fairness.

Our Key Community Projects

A glimpse of the transformative projects carried out by the members of Rotary Bangalore JP Nagar.

Flagship Initiative
Aarohan Medical Fundraiser

Our signature musical fundraising event dedicated to financing critical pediatric heart surgeries, thalassemia treatments, and urgent medical interventions for underprivileged children.

Basic Education
Empowering Young Minds

Distributing comprehensive learning kits, STEM equipment, and educational supplies to government and rural school students across Bengaluru.

Environment
Sustainable & Plastic-Free JP Nagar

Civic awareness drives and mass distribution of reusable eco-friendly bags across local vegetable and shopping markets to reduce single-use plastic waste.
