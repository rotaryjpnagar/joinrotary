Our Community Projects

Over 37 years of tangible impact across child healthcare, government school infrastructure, clean water access, and environmental sustainability in Bengaluru.

Flagship Initiative
Aarohan - Musical Fundraiser

Rotary Bangalore JP Nagar organizes the signature musical fundraiser "Aarohan" to support underprivileged children in urgent need of pediatric heart surgeries, thalassemia treatments, and critical care.

Basic Education
Empowering Young Minds

Annual distribution drives providing comprehensive school kits, notebooks, bags, and learning aids to government school students, fostering educational equity.

Environment
Say No to Plastic

Mass distribution of reusable eco-friendly cloth bags across residential localities and markets in JP Nagar to encourage sustainable community practices.

School Infrastructure
Government School Construction

In partnership with CSR corporate partners, Rotary Bangalore JP Nagar constructed modern school classroom blocks at Nelehal, providing children with safe learning environments.

Healthcare
Dialysis Center Inauguration

Inauguration of modern dialysis machines at CDSIMER, enabling subsidized and free renal care for economically vulnerable kidney patients.

Scholarships
Girl Child Education Sponsorship

Full educational scholarships and annual tuition fee sponsorships by club members to ensure talented young girls complete their higher secondary education.
